Little City: a tiny, hand-drawn town diorama

A tiny, hand-drawn town diorama, free in your browser. Place a few blocks, watch them turn into a town, then watch it come to life.

A house beside a tall tower becomes a chapel. Four blocks stacked by the sea become a lighthouse. Then people move in: the miller carries flour to the bakery, boats go out for fish, and some days there's a market or a wedding on the square. The seasons turn and the lamps come on at dusk.

Or grow a whole town on a new island, a harbour town, a castle town or a farming village, and just watch it.

Works on computers, iPhone, iPad and Android. Made by Derek Yao.
